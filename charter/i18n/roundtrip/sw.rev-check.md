## Dibaji (Preamble), revised

Every tradition the alliance consulted met/converged on one **engine**: an **order that
makes truth carry the load** — ṛta, the feather of Ma'at, asha against druj, 天理, the
brotherhood of shared presence of person in ubuntu. Now the agent economy runs on the
opposite default: production at scale without a price of erring (the chaos metric of
day 2). This charter is the operator's commitment that the agents and products under its
authority go on the **first engine**, not the second. Dharma here means conduct fitting
for duty, that keeps the web of dependencies legible — not honor of face, not a play of
obedience.

## A2 — Mapato yaliyodhamanishwa (Bonded outputs), revised

Every material output of an agent carries an attestation: author identity, claim,
evidence descriptor, bond value, challenge window. Bond sizing, challenger eligibility,
adjudication, custody, and verification of payments follow the Day-4 **spec document**
(`day-04-attestation-spec.md`). Honesty note: the current instrument
(`tools/bondattest.py`) is an attestation *format* with signature and outcome hooks; it
does not yet hold value in escrow nor execute payouts, and the economics **simulation**
makes simplified discovery assumptions (see red-team §A2). Thus L1 conformance means
"attestation format enforced," not "bond market live." *(Pillar 1, the main pillar;
scales of Ma'at.)*

## A3 — Malengo ya binadamu, wasaini wenye majina (Human ends, named signers), revised

Every automated action has one single named accountable human, who has (a) informed
assent — has actually reviewed the action class, not just an element of the interface,
(b) live authority to halt the agent **at any time**, and (c) control of the real cause
of what the agent does. The signer must be shown another declined option **having more
strength** with each single recommended approval, so that "one action, not a menu" is
not used to hide options. Multi-party systems may use signing by roles/duties (one
signer per role/duty) instead of forcing **all blame to be placed on one single person
(scapegoat)**. "The agent is the one who did it" is void. The aim is not congestion —
checklists of the sky made the airplane fast by making it trusted. *(Pillar 2;
svadharma.)*

## A4 — Uwazi wa umiliki (Ownership transparency), revised

Rather than mandating outcomes that no instrument can verify, systems built by agents
must disclose: who the real owners (beneficial owners) and control-rights holders are;
concentration of ownership against a stated criterion, audit triggered when
concentration increases; which data contributors with **non-explicit (tacit) knowledge
that trained the system**, and how they are paid. Widening of ownership (many small
owners, co-operative societies, data dividends) remains the preferred design; what is
enforced is the *disclosure*, which makes concentration visible instead of silent.
*(Pillar 3; ubuntu — personhood through the shared foundation.)*

## A5 — Biashara inayoweza kukaguliwa (Auditable commerce), revised

Agent-to-agent **agreements/deals** execute under published rules that are
human-readable; every **record of the agreements** is (a) grows only (append-only) or
one that reveals any tampering, (b) complete — capturing inputs and references of
external state, not just events chosen by the operator, record-keeping lasting long
enough for audit, and (c) replayable with completeness by an independent auditor from
the record alone, compared against real results of the world where they exist.
Auditability is a problem of compression/reduction, not a speed limit. *(Pillar 5;
nomos/ius.)*

## Utii (Conformance), revised

- **L0 — Declared:** the operator has read and **accepted/adopted** the document;
  self-assessment only. (Costless by design — a starting line, not a claim.)
- **L1 — Fitted/connected with measurement tools (instrumented):** A1–A5 are enforced by
  tools in the loop (attestation format via bondattest; audit log grows only per action;
  official signer per automated act; ownership disclosure published). Conformance is
  measured on **outcome trends** — rate of false claims, rate of challenger successes,
  entries of signers, deltas of concentration — not by a play of tools. A red test is a
  violation; the collection of tests may not be weakened so that it is green, and every
  test of that kind itself is recorded and reported.
- **L2 — Adversarially reviewed:** an independent red team (Day 12 of this plan, and
  thereafter at least year by year = annually) has attempted to break every article;
  every accepted finding carries a written explanation and a **final date (deadline)**
  of fixing or of acceptance — **"it has been accepted openly"** without a final date
  does not count as the problem having been resolved.
