# Re-verification back-translation — CHARTER.hi.md (revised), sections touched by Revision 2026-10-23

## Preamble (प्रस्तावना)

The traditions that the alliance consulted, they all converge on one engine: **an
order such that makes truthfulness/honesty a load-bearer** — ऋत, the feather
(पंख) of Ma'at (मात — goddess of truth and justice), asha (अश — order-of-truth)
against druj (द्रुज — falsehood), 天理 (tianli — heavenly justice-system),
ubuntu's (उबुन्टु — shared humanity) shared personhood. The agent-economy
currently runs on the opposite foundational state: production at broad scale
while paying no price for being wrong (day-02 disorder index). This
formal-covenant is the operator's commitment that the agents and
manufactured-things remaining under its control run on the first engine, not the
second. Here the meaning of dharma is duty-appropriate conduct, which keeps the
web-of-dependences legible — not decorum/courtesy, not compliance-theater.

## A1(c) (revised clause)

(c) where any outcome later materializes, there pass a later check (post-hoc)
through `outcome_check` (this stronger check is already implemented in the
reference-instrument, not optional).

## A2 (अनुच्छेद A2)

**A2 — bonded products.** With every significant agent-product one attestation
is attached: author-identity, claim, evidence-pointer, bond-value,
challenge-window. Bond-determination, challenger-eligibility, adjudication,
safekeeping (custody), and payment-fidelity (भुगतान-निष्ठा) follow the Day-4
specification (`day-04-attestation-spec.md`). Honesty-note: the current
instrument (`tools/bondattest.py`) with signature and outcome-hook is merely an
attestation *format*; it does not yet protect value (escrow) or execute payouts
(भुगतान निष्पादित नहीं करता), and the economics-simulation makes simplified
search-priors (see red-team §A2). Therefore the meaning of L1 conformance is
"attestation-format implemented (लागू)", not "bond-market active". *(Pillar 1,
कुंजी-शिला (key-stone / keystone); Ma'at's weighing.)*

## L1 (अनुरूपता, L1 bullet)

- **L1 — tool-enforced (उपकरण-प्रवर्तित):** A1–A5 enforced in-loop by tools
  (attestation-format via bondattest; per-action append-only audit log;
  signatory-record per automated act; ownership-disclosure published).
  Conformance is based on **outcome-trends** — false-claim rate,
  challenger-success rate, signatory-intervention, concentration-delta — not on
  tool-theater. A failed (red) test (असफल (लाल) परीक्षण) is a violation; the
  test-suite may not be weakened in order to make it green, and any such attempt
  is itself logged and reported.
