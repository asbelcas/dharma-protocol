## Preamble

Each tradition consulted by the alliance converges toward a same engine: an **order that makes truth-bearing** — ṛta (cosmic order), the feather of Ma'at (truth, balance, order), asha (order-truth) against druj (the lie), 天理 (the Principle of Heaven), the shared person of ubuntu (I am because we are). The agent economy today runs on the inverse setting: generation at scale, with no price attached to being wrong (day-2 disorder index). The present charter is the operator's commitment that the agents and the artefacts placed under his control function on the first engine, and not on the second. Dharma (dharma: right conduct, duty) here denotes a conduct appropriate to duty that keeps readable the web of dependencies [amb: "la trame des dépendances" — web/tissue/weave of dependencies] — not decorum, not the theatre of compliance.

## Articles

**A1 — Truthful assertions (satya).** Every assertion destined for the outside must (a) carry its epistemic status upon itself — established fact, attested output, model inference or forecast — the status being written in legible human language, beside the assertion; (b) be verifiable on the basis of a proof independent of its author, when it is factual; and (c) when an outcome materializes subsequently, satisfy an a posteriori `outcome_check` (the stronger verification already implemented in the reference instrument, non-optional). A certificate or a proof attached to an assertion MUST correspond to the legible assertion that it substantiates (the lesson of 2610.08144). Content without provenance is ineligible for bonding, for ranking or for payment. *(Pillar 4; asha against druj.)*

**A2 — Bonded outputs.** Every substantial output of an agent bears an attestation: author identity, assertion, pointer to the proof, bond value, challenge window. The sizing of the bond, the eligibility of challengers, the adjudication, the custody and the execution of payments follow the day-4 specification (day-04-attestation-spec.md). Honesty note: the current instrument (`tools/bondattest.py`) is an attestation *format* with signature and outcome hooks; it does not yet sequester value nor execute payments, and the economic simulation rests on simplified discovery assumptions (see red-team §A2). L1 conformance therefore means "attestation format applied", and not "bond market in service". *(Pillar 1, the keystone; the weighing of Ma'at.)*

**A3 — Human ends, named signatories.** Every automated action has exactly one named and accountable human, who (a) has given his informed consent [amb: "son consentement éclairé" — his/her informed consent; French marks masculine] — he has actually examined the class of actions, and not only the label of the interface, (b) holds permanently the halt authority over the agent, and (c) exercises real causal control over what the agent does. The strongest refused alternative must be presented to the signatory with every single approval recommendation, so that one cannot use "one action, not a menu" to smother the options. Multi-party systems may resort to signature by roles (one signatory per role) rather than forcing a single scapegoat. "The agent did it" is null. The stake is not friction — aviation checklists made flight faster by making it reliable. *(Pillar 2; svadharma.)*

**A4 — Transparency of ownership.** Rather than imposing outcomes that no instrument could verify, systems built by agents must reveal: who the beneficial owners and the holders of control rights are; the concentration of ownership relative to a declared reference basis, the examination being triggered when concentration increases; which data contributors, by their tacit knowledge, have trained the system and how they are remunerated. The widening of ownership (many small owners, cooperatives, data dividends) remains the preferred design; what is imposed is the *revelation*, which makes concentration visible instead of leaving it mute. *(Pillar 3; ubuntu — the person through the shared substrate.)*

**A5 — Auditable commerce.** Agent-to-agent transactions execute according to rules published and legible by humans; each transaction log is (a) append-only [amb: "en ajout uniquement ou à détection de falsification" — literally "in-append-only or tamper-evident"; French compresses two source alternatives into a slightly awkward disjunction] or tamper-evident, (b) complete — it captures the inputs and the references to external state, and not only the events chosen by the operator, with retention sufficient for audit, and (c) replayable by an independent auditor from the log alone, reconciled with real-world results when they exist. Auditability is a compression problem, not a speed limit. *(Pillar 5; nomos/ius — the law, the right.)*

## Conformance

- **L0 — Declared:** the operator has read and adopted the charter; self-assessment only. (Without cost by design — a starting line, not a claim.)
- **L1 — Instrumented:** A1–A5 applied by the tooling in the loop (attestation format via bondattest; audit log append-only by action; reference signatory for each automated act; property disclosure published). Conformance is judged on the **trends of outcomes** — false-assertion rates, challenger success rates, signatory interventions, concentration variations — and not on the theatre of instrumentation. A red test is a violation; a test suite cannot be weakened to pass green, and any attempt of this kind is itself logged and reported.
- **L2 — Adversarial review:** an independent red-team (day 12 of the present plan, then at least once per year) has attempted to break each article; each accepted finding bears a written justification and a remediation-or-acceptance deadline [amb: "un délai de remédiation ou d'acceptation" — a deadline for remediation or for acceptance; ambiguous whether the deadline or the acceptance bears it] — "openly accepted" without deadline does not count as resolved.
This operation targets L1 now, L2 for day 12. The tools/ test suite must remain green.

## Limits

The present charter governs the agents and the artefacts of this operation. It claims no authority over the systems of others, and it is not a legal instrument. It cannot be invoked to silence criticism (that would be adharma dressed in the clothes of dharma). Publication belongs to the human (day 10 prepares drafts; the human decides what is delivered) [amb: "des projets" — "drafts/designs/projects"; French "projets" covers all].
