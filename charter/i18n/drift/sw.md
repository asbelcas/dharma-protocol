## Explanations for non-"none" findings

1. **Derivation "spec"→"strategy":** *mkakati* means strategy/plan; a spec is a fixed specification. Recurs in A2 ("mkakati wa Siku ya 4"). Reader sees a looser, more discretionary artifact than the source asserts. (Also listed under required fixes.)
2. **Derivation "cross-cultural"→"madarasa mbalimbali":** *madarasa* ordinarily means classrooms/classes; "verification of various classes" is puzzling and drops "cultural".
3. **Preamble "engine"→"inji":** nonstandard word (truncation of *injini*); my back-translation first read it as "axle/hinge" — a Swahili reader could likewise misparse the central metaphor.
4. **Preamble "truthfulness"→"uaminifu":** trust/faithfulness rather than truthfulness; slightly shifts the load-bearing virtue from truth to trust.
5. **A2 title "outputs"→"outcomes":** *matokeo* also means outcomes, a loaded term in this charter (outcome_check); risks conceptual bleed.
6. **A2 "pointer"→"descriptor":** a pointer references evidence; a descriptor merely describes it. Slight widening of the attestation field.
7. **A2 "simulation"→"simulizi":** *simulizi* means narration/narrative — wrong near-word; the artifact is mischaracterized as a narrative rather than a simulation. (Also listed under required fixes.)
8. **A3(c) "real causal control"→"control of the real cause":** awkward parse in Swahili; meaning approximately recoverable but stilted.
9. **A3 "strongest"→comparative:** superlative lost; the requirement to show the strongest declined alternative is softened.
10. **A3 "scapegoat"→"mbuzi wa kufungwa" (material):** the idiom was decomposed literally into "goat" + "shut away". The sense — do not centralize all blame/authority on one blame-bearer — is unrecoverable from the Swahili text; the clause reads as nonsense about an animal. Swahili has no native idiom for "scapegoat", but the correct handling is a sense-based rendering with an English gloss (see untranslatable terms). (Listed under required fixes.)
11. **A3 "friction"→"msongamano":** congestion/cramming, not process drag; with "sky checklists" for "aviation's checklists" the rhetorical point survives only approximately.
12. **A4 "tacit"→"secret":** *maarifa ya siri* = confidential knowledge; tacit (uncodified, unspoken) knowledge is a different disclosure target.
13. **A4 "trained"→"cultivated":** *yaliyolimia* (from *kulima*, to farm) is a nonstandard, inventive verb form; "knowledge that farmed the system" confuses.
14. **A5 "deals"→"contracts":** *mikataba* tightens "deals" to formal contracts; a reader may think informal dealings are out of scope.
15. **A5 "deal log"→"plan record":** *mpango* means plan/agenda, not deal; "kumbukumbu ya mpango" misnames the ledger of record.
16. **L0 "adopted"→"used":** *kuitumia* is weaker than adopting a charter; conformance posture sounds more casual.
17. **L1 title "Instrumented":** the term tying L1 to "instrumentation theater" is lost; the level reads as "enforced with tools".
18. **L2 "deadline"→"date":** *tarehe* reads as the date a fix happened, not a by-when; the enforcement teeth of accepted findings are softened.
19. **L2 "'openly accepted'"→"'wazi kukubaliwa'":** parses as "open to be accepted", inverting the intended status label.
20. **Limits "artifacts"→"products":** *bidhaa* narrows the governed class to commercial products.

## Untranslatable-term notes

Terms Kiswahili genuinely lacks a native equivalent for, and how the translation handled them:

- **scapegoat** — no native idiom for "blame-bearer". Handled by literal decomposition into *mbuzi wa kufungwa* (penned goat): **failed**; should be sense-rendered with a gloss (e.g. *"lawama ziwekezwe kwa mtu mmoja (scapegoat)"*).
- **tacit (tacit knowledge)** — no common adjective for "unspoken/uncodified". Handled as *maarifa ya siri* (secret knowledge): distorts meaning.
- **friction** (process sense) — no established metaphor. Handled as *msongamano* (congestion): imperfect.
- **instrumented / instrumentation** — no term for "fitted with measurement probes". Handled as *kwa zana* (with tools): acceptable paraphrase, term-link to "instrumentation theater" lost.
- **engine** (converges on one engine) — calqued as *inji* (nonstandard truncation of *injini*): ambiguous.
- **append-only / tamper-evident / replayable** — no native terms. Handled by literal calque plus English gloss in parentheses: **good practice**.
- **beneficial owners** — kept as English token with Swahili gloss *wamiliki halisi (beneficial owners)*: **good practice**.
- **outcome_check** — preserved as a bare token per the key-terms header: **good practice**.
- **keystone / substrate / decorum** — no direct nouns; handled by metaphor substitution (*nguzo kuu* main pillar, *msingi wa pamoja* shared foundation, *heshima ya sura* honor of face): acceptable.
